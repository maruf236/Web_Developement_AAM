    Everybody loves to listen to music.
The types of songs people listen to vary according to
their tastes, ages, and sometimes genders.

I also love listening to music. Most of the time,
I listen to Hindi, English, and Bangla music. Apart
from songs, I sometimes enjoy listening to instrumental 
tunes played on the piano, violin, and flute. When I am 
going to sleep, I like listening to the sounds of rain or the ocean.

Music soothes our minds and calms our bodies. I believe 
that, depending on our surroundings, our brains receive different 
types of frequencies that influence our moods and preferences. 
Therefore, a person's taste in music can sometimes reveal their 
personality and characteristics. Here, I am going to present some 
music that I listen to from time to time.
